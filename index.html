CYBERJOURNEY ROADMAP

A Progressive Learning Architecture

1. Cyber Academy (Beginner)

Learn core cybersecurity fundamentals: password policies, phishing detection, malware awareness, safe browsing habits, and device hardening.

2. CyberOps Lab (Intermediate)

Train as a Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst: dissect threat logs, inspect network PCAPs, perform digital forensics, and respond to incidents.

3. Cipher Escape (Advanced)

Master advanced cryptanalysis: escape cryptographic rooms by solving classic ciphers, public key encryption puzzles, and hash breakdown challenges.
